KAROLINA OBIDIKE — PROFILE SITE

Open index.html in a browser to view the profile. Open projects.html for the projects page.

To change your content:
- Edit text and links in index.html and projects.html.
- Replace images/profile-pic.jpg with your preferred photo, keeping the same filename.
- The site styling and responsive layout are in profileStyles.css.
- The entrance replay button is controlled by script.js.
