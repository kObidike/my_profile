02 / Product notes

What sits behind it.

01

The idea

Bring first-party news, exchange announcements and market context into one research flow, while keeping the public feed deliberately selective.

02

The approach

Separate public reading from the owner workspace, keep an evidence trail for each item and make room for review, tuning and measured outcomes.

03

What stays private

The source code, admin tools, scoring rules, live integrations, production data and configuration are not part of this portfolio preview.
